Is ORSPEX Business Suite a subscription?

No. It's a one-time purchase of $50. You own the software forever and receive free updates for 12 months.

Does ORSPEX Business Suite work offline?

Yes. It runs entirely on your Windows PC with no internet connection required. Your business data stays on your computer — no cloud, no server, no subscription.

What is included in ORSPEX Business Suite?

Full double-entry accounting, multi-warehouse inventory, manufacturing with BOM, sales and invoicing, purchasing and suppliers, and VAT-ready tax handling.

Is the ORSPEX PDF Compressor really free?

Yes. The ORSPEX PDF Compressor is completely free with no trial, no watermark, and no signup. Use it online in your browser or download the Windows app. It's open source on GitHub.

What's the difference between the online and offline versions?

The online version runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript — no uploads, quick metadata and structure cleanup. The Windows app uses Ghostscript for maximum compression on scanned and image-heavy PDFs. Both are free.

Do I need Ghostscript for the Windows app?

Yes. The Windows app uses Ghostscript (free) to do the compression. Install it once, then the app handles everything.

Can ORSPEX compress a PDF to under 1 MB?

Yes. The Windows app includes a target-size mode. Enter 1 MB and the app automatically finds the best settings to bring the file under that limit.
