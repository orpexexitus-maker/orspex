What is the ORSPEX Business Companion?

A set of companion utilities and downloadable resources that work alongside the ORSPEX Business Suite. Free for Suite owners.

Is the ORSPEX PDF Compressor really free?

Yes. Completely free, no trial, no watermark, no signup. Use it online in your browser or download the Windows app.
